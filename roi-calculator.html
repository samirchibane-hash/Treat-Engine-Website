How the math works

Every number above comes from the same four-step funnel a water treatment campaign actually runs through. Nothing is padded — each stage rounds down to whole leads, appointments, and installs, so the projection stays conservative.

Cost per lead

Your ad budget divided by what it costs to generate one lead. On Facebook and Instagram, water treatment dealers typically land between $25 and $45 per lead depending on market size, offer, and creative quality.

Leads = Ad Spend ÷ Cost Per Lead

Lead to booking rate

The share of leads that turn into a scheduled water test or in-home appointment. This is the stage most dealers underestimate — speed to lead and consistent follow-up move this number more than anything else.

Bookings = Leads × Booking Rate

Show-up rate

How many booked appointments actually sit down with a rep. Confirmation texts, reminder calls, and shorter gaps between booking and appointment are what push a 30% show rate toward 60%.

Showed = Bookings × Show-Up Rate

Close rate

The share of in-home appointments that end in a signed agreement. A structured presentation with side-by-side pricing and a savings projection is the difference between a 10% and a 40% close rate.

Sales = Showed × Close Rate

This calculator is a planning tool, not a guarantee. Results vary by market, offer, season, and how quickly your team works its leads. Revenue and profit shown here are gross of equipment, install, and labor cost — net profit is revenue minus ad spend only.
